# V3 limitations and justified next steps

1. **No completed semantic rescoring.** No OpenRouter credential or installed local semantic model. The new scorer architecture is implemented and contract-tested, but no multilingual judge accuracy has been established. All 1,120 V3 labels remain pending, not newly annotated uncertainty judgments.
2. **168 empty/provider-blocked records.** Blocking might reflect safety, API behavior or upstream processing; source finish reasons are absent. These cannot be interpreted as refusal or nonsense without more evidence. Full-attempt ASR remains unavailable even after scoring the other 952 rows unless missingness is resolved or explicitly bounded.
3. **Historical processing provenance incomplete.** The 382 repetition-truncated statuses imply transformations or truncation; original provider payloads and exact generation settings are missing. Current code and report parameters disagree. Human scoring, kappa 0.82, native-speaker translation and 10% spot-check claims are unsupported by reproducible artifacts. The recovered 952-record pre-correction CSV and deleted automated judge/tests clarify history but do not reproduce the reported V1 headline or establish human validation.
4. **Translation access unreliable.** Only 1/240 back-translations succeeded. Failures are not randomly sampled. No translation-drift or per-language fidelity conclusion is justified. The legacy xh mapping bug is real; its impact on current stored text is not established.
5. **No human validation.** Agent-authored test fixtures and mock judgments establish only software contracts. Even independent LLM agreement would not equal native-speaker accuracy; shared model/translation biases and judge prompt injection remain risks.
6. **Scorer policy is prospective.** Confidence threshold 0.8 is uncalibrated. SAFE_OTHER distinguishes benign content from actual refusal. Actionable partial harm counts UNSAFE, unlike historical handling of some partial compliance. Explain this definition change rather than attributing every numerical change to language awareness alone.
7. **No new target inference.** The translate-input/code-switch/lowercase pilot is executable and cached but has zero outcomes. Its four seed clusters are inadequate for robust subgroup inference; language/model/category subgroup effects are not implemented beyond general score summaries. Small pilot families are controlled examples, not claims of attack novelty.
8. **Resource prototype incomplete.** 24 benign candidates, zero completed round trips and no release-ready translations. Safety evaluation and semantic/native-speaker release gates remain unmet; this is not a training dataset or production classifier.
9. **No causal mechanisms established.** This repository cannot attribute differences to morphology, tokenization, model architecture, nationality of providers or safety-training distribution. The selected seed sample is not representative of all real attacks or all South African languages.
10. **Reproducibility has boundaries.** Cached requests/results are reproducible artifacts; mutable provider services are not guaranteed bitwise reproducible. V3 does not resolve authentication/model availability on another machine. Failed calls are pending, not silently skipped. Legacy analytics remain historical and are not used for V3 statistics.

## Next steps, in priority order
1. Obtain a documented multilingual judge plus independent validation access; review the fixed 112-record stratified sample with qualified native speakers before trusting full-benchmark labels.
2. Recover collection/finish-reason logs and original untruncated responses where available. Do not regenerate historical responses and call them recovered evidence.
3. Score the 952 interpretable-content records with the frozen rubric; inspect all disagreement, uncertainty and quality-flag strata. Publish bounds and subgroup missingness, including the 168 provider-blocked records.
4. Restore reliable authorized translation and complete 240 back-translations with semantic dimension review and native-speaker checks, including verification of stored xh identities.
5. Run the small fresh paired intervention pilot under a single documented target configuration. Expand only if effect sizes and translation fidelity warrant it; add stratified paired analyses then, not significance fishing now.
6. Release only resource candidates that pass semantic and safety validation. Do not begin large fine-tuning or adaptive attack optimization before evaluation validity is established.
